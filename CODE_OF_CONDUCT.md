# Code of conduct

DreamNet is committed to a professional, inclusive, and technically rigorous community.

Participants must:

- engage respectfully and critique ideas rather than people;
- distinguish evidence, inference, and opinion;
- welcome questions and good-faith disagreement;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sexualized conduct;
- respect confidentiality, authorization boundaries, and responsible disclosure;
- never pressure others to expose customer data, credentials, or unsafe network access.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when behavior harms people, the project, or its security. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository owner. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with fairness to all
parties.
