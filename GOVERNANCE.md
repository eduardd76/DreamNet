# Governance

DreamNet uses a maintainer-led governance model during its experimental phase.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers decide project direction, review and merge contributions, manage releases, enforce the
code of conduct, and protect the safety boundaries of the project. Technical decisions should favor
reproducible evidence over popularity or vendor preference.

## Decision principles

1. Network correctness and operator safety come before autonomy.
2. Claims require reproducible tests and explicit limitations.
3. Analysis autonomy must remain separate from authority to change a network.
4. Deterministic validation is preferred for facts that can be checked mechanically.
5. Interfaces should stay vendor-neutral while adapters may be vendor-specific.
6. Backward compatibility matters after a public schema is declared stable.

## Becoming a maintainer

Regular contributors may be invited after demonstrating sustained, constructive participation,
sound engineering judgment, and respect for the project's safety boundaries. Maintainer access is
granted by the project owner and may be withdrawn for inactivity, security risk, or code-of-conduct
violations.

## Changes to governance

Material governance changes should be proposed in a public issue and remain open for community
comment before the owner makes the final decision.
